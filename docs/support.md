# Kid Money support

For private help or questions, email
[kidmoneyledger@duck.com](mailto:kidmoneyledger@duck.com). For non-sensitive bug
reports or feature requests, you may also [open an issue in the public Kid Money
repository](https://github.com/ejones23/kid-money/issues).

Please do not send children's names, balances, transaction history, device logs
containing personal information, or other private information by email or in a
public issue. Describe the behavior using fictional names and amounts instead.

The project source and current development status are available in the [Kid Money repository](https://github.com/ejones23/kid-money).
